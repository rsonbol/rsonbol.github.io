Courses Taught by Dr. Riad Sonbol

Generative AI and LLMs (Syrian Private University)

Faculty of Engineering - Artificial Intelligence Engineering

[Last Updated: 2025] This course offers a comprehensive introduction to the principles and techniques behind Generative AI and Large Language Models (LLMs). Students will explore the foundations of modern NLP systems, starting with embeddings and progressing to the transformer architecture that powers today’s most advanced models.
